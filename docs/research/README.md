# Research reports

Literature searches run on 2026-08-04, after an audit found fabricated
citations across the blog and in SCIENCE.md. The brief for all of them was
the same: find the source, do not report its absence, and never cite a paper
you have not opened.

They are kept because they contain the reading, the DOIs, the quoted
passages and the search routes. SCIENCE.md carries the conclusions; these
carry the working.

A fifth report, on the normative priors, was lost when parallel agents wrote
to the same scratch directory. Its finding survives because it was checked
independently against the published PDF before being applied: the 1-5 priors
now come from Kajonius & Johnson (2019) Table A1, N = 320,128, and the
reasoning is in `SCIENCE.md` and in the header of `api/scoring.py`.

## Reading them

Treat them as evidence, not as conclusions. Each was written by an agent and
every load-bearing claim in them was re-checked before anything was changed
in the product. Two claims did not survive that check and are not acted on:

- That the authors of the IPIP-NEO-120 norms describe their Openness sample
  as unrepresentatively high. The discussion section was read in full looking
  for it and it is not there.
- That Bell (2007) reports team mean Openness at ρ = .00 in field settings.
  Her published abstract says the opposite, and the results tables are
  paywalled, so the point is recorded in SCIENCE.md as unsettled rather than
  decided in either direction.

The lesson those two carry is the one the whole exercise is about: a
secondary report of what a paper says is not the paper.
